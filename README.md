# IT Support Labs
### Kathleen Kuhlman

This repository will document my hands-on practice in IT support,
troubleshooting, operating systems, and networking as I prepare
for entry-level IT opportunities.

## Portfolio Status

I am setting up this portfolio. Completed lab write-ups will be
added as I work through practical exercises.

## Documentation Approach

Each lab write-up will explain:
- The objective and environment
- The steps I performed and why
- Any problems encountered and how I investigated them
- The results and how I verified them
- What I learned

All activities will be performed on systems I own or am
authorized to use. Screenshots and examples will exclude
passwords, credentials, and personal information.
